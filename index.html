01 The Centre

An eye hospital built around how patients actually feel.

Sun Eye Hospital is a dedicated eye-care centre in Goharbaug, Bilimora. We were set up around a simple belief: that good eye care begins with listening — understanding what you are experiencing before anything else happens.

That means appointments that are not rushed, findings explained in words you can follow, and a care plan that is discussed with you rather than handed to you. Our team, our surroundings and our processes are all shaped by that one idea.

Whether you are visiting for a routine check-up, a second opinion or ongoing care, you will find the same approach: clear, calm and personal.

02 Care Principles

Four commitments that shape every visit.

01

Unhurried consultations

Appointments are kept generously paced so there is genuine time to talk, ask questions and understand what is happening with your eyes.

02

Plain-language explanation

Findings and options are described in everyday terms — no jargon, no pressure — so you can make a decision you actually feel confident about.

03

Care shaped around you

Every plan begins with your situation, your comfort and your priorities — not a one-size-fits-all checklist.

04

Continuity across visits

Your care is followed by the same team over time, so your next visit builds on the last instead of starting from scratch.

About Us

Care that takes the time to explain.

Sun Eye Hospital is an eye-care centre on Convent Road in Goharbaug, Bilimora. We opened with a straightforward intention: to make good eye care feel calm, clear and personal for the people of Bilimora and the surrounding towns.

Eye concerns carry a particular kind of worry — they touch how you read, work, drive and see the people around you. We try to meet that worry with patience first, and technology second.

01 Our Story

Built for a town that deserves better access.

For many families around Bilimora, specialist eye care has meant travelling to larger cities — often more than once, often with a long wait in between. Sun Eye Hospital was founded so that a good part of that journey could happen closer to home.

The hospital was designed deliberately: quiet spaces, natural light, and a layout that keeps the patient, rather than the equipment, at the centre of the room.

02 What to Expect

Three steps, every time.

No surprises. Here is exactly how a first visit to Sun Eye Hospital unfolds.

01

Consultation

We begin by listening. You describe what you have noticed, how long it has been happening, and what matters most to you. Nothing is rushed.

02

Evaluation

A focused examination follows, using the diagnostic tools relevant to your concern. Findings are shown and explained to you as we go.

03

Care Plan

You leave with a clear plan: what we recommend, what the alternatives are, and when to come back. Questions are welcome at any point.

03 What We Value

The things we refuse to compromise on.

Patient comfort

Quiet rooms, gentle handling and appointments that are not overcrowded. Comfort is part of care, not a bonus.

Clarity

You should never leave a consultation unsure about what was found or what happens next.

Personalised attention

Two people with the same condition rarely need the same plan. We treat the person, not the diagnosis.

Local presence

We are from and of Bilimora. Being reachable, accountable and nearby matters as much as the technology inside.
