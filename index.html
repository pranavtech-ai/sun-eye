About Sun Eye Hospital

An eye hospital built around how patients actually feel.

Sun Eye Hospital is an eye-care centre on Convent Road in Goharbaug, Bilimora. The approach is simple: make good eye care feel calm, clear and personal for the people of Bilimora and the surrounding towns.

Close to home.Specialist eye care designed to feel less rushed and more understandable.
What matters here

Four commitments that shape every visit.

The experience is designed around the patient first — before the equipment, before the terminology, and before the treatment plan.

Unhurried consultations

Time to understand the concern, ask questions and talk through what the findings mean.

↗

Plain-language explanation

The Sun Eye approach

Patience first. Technology second.

Eye concerns can affect how you read, work, drive and see the people around you. The goal is to meet that worry with patience, clarity and a thoughtful next step.

Patient comfort

Quiet, considered interactions throughout the visit.

Clarity

Services

Eye care, organised around what you need.

A focused service mix covering common vision concerns, ongoing eye health and comprehensive assessment.

Cataract Evaluation & Care

Assessment of lens clouding and a clear, unhurried discussion of the options available to you.

↗

LASIK & Refractive Care

Evaluation for people who would like to reduce their dependence on spectacles or contact lenses.

↗

Glaucoma Care

Regular monitoring and long-term follow-up for raised eye pressure and optic nerve health.

↗

Retina Services

Eye care focused on retinal health and the changes that need to be assessed and followed over time.

↗

Comprehensive Eye Examination

A complete check of your vision and eye health — the right starting point for almost every concern.

↗

Diabetic Eye Check-up

Assessment focused on retinal blood vessels and changes that may be worth tracking over time.

↗
